About

I'm an industrial engineer focused on process improvement, operations analysis and data-driven decision making. I enjoy turning messy, real-world workflows into clear, measurable systems, and working with teams to make the improvements stick.

I grew up in Johannesburg and started a Business Science degree in Analytics at the University of Cape Town before moving to Israel. There I served as a combat soldier in the IDF Paratroopers, then completed a BSc in Industrial Engineering and Information Systems at Bar-Ilan University. Today I work remotely as a project officer and analyst for an early-stage gold exploration company in Côte d'Ivoire.
